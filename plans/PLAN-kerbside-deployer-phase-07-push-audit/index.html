    PLAN: Kerbside deployer phase 7 -- push audit¶

    Planning effort: medium. Review effort: medium.

    Why this phase exists¶

    Phases 1 to 6 landed in six pull requests here and five in two other repositories, over four weeks. Each was reviewed on its own terms. This phase runs PUSH-AUDIT.md over all of them together, which is the only reading that can see what the phases did to each other.

    The plan's shape invites that kind of defect. Phase 2 decided what Shaken Fist does when kerbside_url is set and no kerbside group exists. Phase 3 decided what site.yml does in the same case, and phase 5 rewrote most of phase 3's plays when the role moved to Kerbside. Phase 1 taught internal_ca to issue a certificate to any host, and phase 3 was its first caller outside allsf. Every one of those seams is a place where each side's review could be right and the pair wrong, and the tokens plan's audit found exactly that shape of defect -- a blocking, destructive one -- in the configuration no phase had tested. It recommended that the seam lens become a standing heading for cross-repository plans, so this plan adds it (decision 6).

    There is also an open report from inside this plan's feature. #4598 records Kerbside refusing a freshly minted console token with 401 invalid token signature, thirteen minutes after the role's "wait for signing keys" task passed, in the lane phase 4 built. Nobody has diagnosed it. The audit does not fix it, but the seam heading reads the signing-key fetch with that report in hand (survey finding 7).

    Scope¶

    In scope.

    • The six merges in decision 1, under every PUSH-AUDIT.md heading: wave 1, the wave 2 mechanical sweep, the style judgment portion, and 2a to 2d.
    • The five merges in Kerbside and actions that phases 5 and 6 recorded, under those repositories' own runbooks (decision 2). Read-only.
    • Two headings of this plan's own: the cross-repository seam lens (step 7i) and the plan-push-audit-phase five-item floor applied to the cross-repository ranges, with the removed-consumer greps stated (step 7j).
    • The master plan's Future work entries that have no issue (survey finding 9), and the four seeded candidates S1 to S4. Disposition decides what each becomes.

    Out of scope.

    • Fixing what the audit finds. Decision 8 says where each kind of finding goes.
    • Editing Kerbside or actions from this phase. Findings there become issues in those repositories (decision 2), and the operator pushes any actions change, by the master plan's precedent.
    • The later merges that touch audited files (survey finding 8). They matter only where they change whether a finding still holds, which decision 3 covers.
    • Diagnosing #4598. The seam heading says what it can about the fetch path; the issue keeps the diagnosis.
    • Waiting for the weekly lane. Survey finding 6 explains; decision 13 says what the test review uses instead.
