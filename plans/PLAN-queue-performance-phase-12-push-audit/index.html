    PLAN: Queue performance phase 12 -- push audit of phases 9 to 11¶

    Planning effort: medium. Review effort: medium.

    Why this phase exists¶

    Phase 8 ran PUSH-AUDIT.md over phases 1 to 8 and found that coalescing had never worked. Phases 9, 10 and 11 then reopened the plan and changed the same code again: phase 9 proved the fold and instrumented it, phase 10 instrumented where pre-execution time goes, and phase 11 widened the coalescing key to two columns and rewrote its guards twice before it merged. Nothing has audited those three phases as one body of work. Phase 8 cannot have, because none of them existed when it ran, and it stays where it is and goes on saying what it covered (see "Phase 12" in the master plan).

    This plan's defects have been ones that phases did to each other. Phase 8 found a join that phases 4 and 5 had each written correctly on their own terms. Phase 8's own fix would have activated a cross-node fold, and only review caught it. This audit exists to ask the same question of phases 9 to 11.

    Scope¶

    In scope. The five merges which put phases 9 to 11 on develop, audited under every PUSH-AUDIT.md heading: wave 1, the wave 2 mechanical sweep, the style judgment portion, and 2a to 2d. Also one heading of this plan's own, the coherence of the coalescing rule (step 12h), which the master plan names as the first area worth looking at.

    Out of scope.

    • Phases 1 to 8, which phase 8 audited. Where a phase 9 to 11 change relies on something earlier, the dependency is noted, not re-audited.

    • 3916 (issue-fix-3863, the sf-queues dependency-wait back-off).¶

      Phase 10's premise rests on it, but the autofixer landed it for #3863, it was reviewed in its own pull request, and the plan's Merged column does not claim it.
    • The ten later commits on develop which touch the audited files (listed in survey finding 4). They are not this plan's work. They matter only where they change whether a finding is still true, which decision 2 covers.
    • Fixing what the audit finds. Decision 7 says what happens to each kind of finding and which pull request carries the fix.

    What the survey found¶

    The master plan's Phase 12 section was written on 2026-09-10, after phase 11 merged, so it is less stale than most phase sections. Its baseline is correct. Four of its other claims were wrong or have gone out of date, and the survey found one gap that phase 11 left. The false claims are corrected at source in this commit, in the master plan's Status and Phase 12 sections and in the docs/plans/index.md row. A later step does not need to redo that.

    1. The baseline is right, and every merge is on develop. git merge-base --is-ancestor <sha> origin/develop holds for all five: 30168f0dd (#3905), 9e41f07c6 (#3951), fea4af7bd (#3973), 9aab98d94 (#4007), ff65c622a (#4033). Each one's first-parent diff is confined to coalescing, queue and instrumentation files, plus plan documents and generated protos.

    2. git log over the #4033 range is wrong, and git diff is right. The queue-performance-phase-11-review-followup branch merged develop into itself mid-flight. As a result, git log ff65c622a^1..ff65c622a lists about forty unrelated commits (renovate bumps, scheduler-reservations phase 7, agent-operation-deadlines phase 7). Only three commits are this plan's: e27601c222, 082d85c56c and 61d262d4ca. The first-parent diff is clean, at 20 files, all of them coalescing work. An auditor who reads commits rather than the diff would audit other plans' work under this plan's name.

    3. #4033 is not a "measurement write-up". The master plan's Status called it one. In fact it answered the automated review of #4007, which merged at its original head before that review was addressed, and it carried code. It added _reusable_priorities() (shakenfist/schema/operations/net_op.py:156), which makes the enqueue-side dedup priority-aware through a new proto field and an IN filter. It added the missing_key_columns guard and its key_column_missing outcome (shakenfist/operations/baseoperation.py:546-577). It also added the upgrade-step tests, and recorded step 11h's measurement. That is 1,778 insertions. Corrected at source.

Since then, #4033 added two more, and the original list had already left several out. Step 12h's brief lists all eleven.

    Corrected during execution: there are eleven, not ten. 8420816261 (#4303, "Reap and never adopt rowless cluster operations") changed _direct_find_existing_coalescible_op and daemons/database/main.py. The survey missed it because its git log was not scoped to mariadb.py. 12d and 12g both found it, and it touches neither routing nor the fold.

    Decisions¶

    1. The baseline is the five merge ranges, read with git diff and never with git log. Each range is <sha>^1..<sha>:
    Phase PR Range
    9 #3905 30168f0dd^1..30168f0dd
    9 #3951 9e41f07c6^1..9e41f07c6
    10 #3973 fea4af7bd^1..fea4af7bd
    11 #4007 9aab98d94^1..9aab98d94
    11 #4033 ff65c622a^1..ff65c622a

    Wave 1's greps and the wave 2 mechanical sweep run once per range, and their output is pooled. That is the runbook's rule: git cannot express the union of these five as one range. git log is excluded because of survey finding 2. Any agent that needs commit messages for context is given the three #4033 commits by SHA.
